FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Will free study material remain available?

Yes. The site can contain both free and premium resources.

How will premium PDFs be delivered?

Initially, the planned flow is UPI payment → customer submits UTR/transaction ID → Himalayan PathShala verifies the payment → approved customer receives access to the protected resource.

Can the same question bank be reused?

Yes. The planned question bank is reusable across multiple tests and exam profiles.

Can exam rules be different for different tests?

Yes. Exam Profiles are designed specifically for this: question count, subject distribution, duration and marking rules can be configured separately.

Will there be a customer account?

That is part of the next backend stage, where customers can see purchases, tests and available downloads.
