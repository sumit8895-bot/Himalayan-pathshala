FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Will free study material remain available?

Yes. The site can contain both free and premium resources.

How will premium PDFs be delivered?

Initially: UPI payment → UTR submission → Himalayan PathShala verification → protected download access.

Can the same question bank be reused?

Yes. The question bank is designed to be reusable across multiple tests and exam profiles.

Can exam rules be different for different tests?

Yes. Exam Profiles can have separate question counts, subject distribution, duration and marking rules.
